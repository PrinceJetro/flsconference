Advancing Global Health Frontiers

The One Health Mission

The "One Health" approach recognizes that the health of people is closely connected to the health of animals and our shared environment.

This conference serves as a critical nexus for researchers to share innovative strategies in pandemic preparedness, genomic surveillance, and ecological conservation.
