About the Conference

Uniting diverse scientific disciplines to address the complex interconnectedness of human, animal, and environmental health at the University of Lagos.

Legacy of Excellence

Established with a vision to pioneer biological research in West Africa, the Faculty of Life Sciences at the University of Lagos has been at the forefront of scientific discovery for decades. Our commitment to academic rigor and societal impact has produced generations of leaders in genetics, microbiology, and environmental biology.

As we navigate the 21st century, the Faculty has evolved into a powerhouse of innovation. From tackling infectious diseases to developing sustainable biotechnologies, our research centers are hubs of multi-disciplinary collaboration that bridge the gap between theory and application.
