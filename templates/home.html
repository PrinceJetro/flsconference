Inaugural Conference

Focus of FLS One Health 2026

The Inaugural Annual Conference of the Faculty of Life Sciences, University of Lagos brings together scholars, industry leaders, and policy frameworks around the **"One Health"** paradigm.

With the rapid shift in planetary climates and urbanization, identifying transmission cycles, heavy metal bio-accumulation pathways, and biological safety mechanisms becomes imperative. This conference targets research outputs that drive real-world application, translating laboratory discovery into life-saving policies.

Read our mission statement arrow_forward

Scientific Sub-themes & Tracks

coronavirus

Emerging & Re-emerging Infectious Diseases

Epidemiological surveillance, host-pathogen interactions, vector control, and novel disease outbreaks.

dna

Non-Communicable Diseases: Monogenic & Complex Traits

Molecular genetics, heredity analysis, complex biological traits, and genomic screening methods.

pets

Zoonotic Spillover in Wildlife–Livestock Contact Zones

Ecological interfaces, animal-to-human transmission barriers, wildlife preservation, and buffer zones.

vaccines

Antimicrobial Resistance at the Human–Animal–Environment Interface

Development of resistance profiles, ecological runoffs, veterinary stewardship, and community hygiene.

eco

Conservation, Climate Change & Environmental Health

Ecosystem stability, carbon footprint modeling, biodiversity preservation, and planetary health.

query_stats

Forensics, Integrated Data Systems & Bio-surveillance

Molecular forensics, integrated data models, bio-surveillance platforms, and security networks.

pill

Drug Discovery & Design

Phyto-medicine, pharmaceutical biochemistry, molecular docking, and drug design frameworks.

security

Food Security, Biosecurity & Safety

Sustainable agriculture, biosecurity barriers, food preservation, and nutritional safety.
